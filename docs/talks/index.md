## OSPOlogy Asia 2026 — Tokyo

BomLens: An Open-Source, One-Click SBOM Tool for Supply Chain Security and Regulatory Compliance

- **Event:** [OSPOlogy Asia 2026 (Linux Foundation)](https://community.linuxfoundation.org/events/details/lfhq-ospology-asia-presents-ospology-asia/)
- **Date:** 27 July 2026
- **Location:** Tokyo, Japan
- **Format:** 30-minute live demo

A live run that turns folders, Git repos, received SBOMs, and AI models into SBOMs, and checks them for vulnerabilities, licenses, and regulatory conformance.

## Open Source Supply Chain Security Tools — OpenChain Korea Work Group

BomLens, Trusted OSS, and TRUSCA: how a rejected SBOM gets fixed on the supplier side, and how AI-coding-era governance gets enforced on the receiving side.

- **Event:** [OpenChain Korea Work Group, 31st Meeting](https://openchain-project.github.io/OpenChain-KWG/)
- **Date:** 8 September 2026
- **Format:** 25-minute session

Two problems, two answers: SBOMs that get rejected before they're even usable, met by BomLens; policies that never get enforced once AI agents start adding dependencies on their own, met by Trusted OSS and TRUSCA.
