## OSPOlogy Asia 2026 — Tokyo

BomLens: An Open-Source, One-Click SBOM Tool for Supply Chain Security and Regulatory Compliance

- **Event:** OSPOlogy Asia 2026 (Linux Foundation)
- **Date:** 27 July 2026
- **Location:** Tokyo, Japan
- **Format:** 30-minute live demo
- **Speaker:** Haksung Jang, OSPO Manager, SK Telecom

The talk opens with why the tool exists: SK Telecom asked its suppliers for an SBOM, and many suppliers had no way to produce one. The rest is a live run. One folder becomes a full SBOM with a vulnerability, dependency, and license read. A project with no lockfile — reached by a bare GitHub URL — still resolves its transitive dependencies at exact versions. SBOMs received from a supplier, in both SPDX and CycloneDX, are checked for completeness and for known-exploitable vulnerabilities. An AI model becomes an ML-BOM measured against the G7 minimum elements, with a crosswalk to the EU AI Act and Korea's AI Framework Act. Finally the standard SBOM is handed to a governance portal, closing the loop that opened the talk: a customer's regulation becomes a supplier's request to "send us an SBOM."
